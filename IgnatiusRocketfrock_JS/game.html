Ignatius Rocketfrock

Move with ←/→ or A/D. Jump with ↑, W, or Z. Press jump again for the double-jump kick, then hold to hover-slowfall. Mouse/touch: press anywhere in the playfield, drag left/right to run, drag up to jump/boost, and quick double-tap to launch the homing rocket. Rocket fuel only recharges after landing. Space, X, K, or either Ctrl key also launches the rocket.
